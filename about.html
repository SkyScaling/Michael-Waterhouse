Michael Waterhouse

President · Waterhouse & Associates

Hi, I'm Mike. I help self-employed people and business owners across Ontario protect the income that everything else depends on.

I came at this from the health side first. My degree is in Health Sciences, and my early career was clinical work and future cost of care assessments. That meant sitting down with someone after a serious injury and putting a real number on what the rest of their life was going to cost. Care, equipment, lost earnings, all of it. I did that math for a living.

From there I moved to the financial side, first in employee benefits and pension management, then back to working directly with personal injury clients, supporting them through the claims process and long after it was over. I saw which people had something in place before it happened and which ones didn't. The gap between those two groups is not small.

Today I lead Waterhouse & Associates. We work alongside a network of estate and tax specialists, lawyers and accountants, so nothing gets looked at in isolation. Some clients come to us with an advisory team already in place and we work with it. Others need us to bring the right people in.

So when I talk about what happens if your income stops, I'm not asking you to imagine it. I've watched it happen to a lot of people, and I've been the one who priced it out.

Outside of work I'm usually on the water sailing, on a golf course, or coaching and playing volleyball. Mostly though, I'm with my family.

On our call I'll ask about your work, your income, and what you already have in place. Then I'll show you where you stand and what it would cost to close any gaps. If you're already properly covered, I'll tell you and let you get on with your day.

My aim is that you leave that call feeling clear, understood, and in control. No pressure, no jargon, no obligation.
