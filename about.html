Michael Waterhouse

President · Waterhouse & Associates

Hi, I'm Mike. I help individuals, business owners and corporations across Canada understand the true cost of disability, and the real value of life insurance and living benefits.

My background combines an education in Health Sciences with decades of experience in disability management and financial services. My past work has included future cost of care assessments, employee benefits, pension management, and supporting personal injury clients through their claims and beyond.

That experience gives me a clear perspective: I know what disability truly costs and what proper coverage is worth. Not in theory. In numbers.

Today I lead Waterhouse & Associates and work with a network of licensed advisors, accountants and lawyers specializing in living benefits, life insurance, and tax and estate planning.

On our call I'll ask about your work, your income, and what you already have in place. Then I'll show you where you stand and the steps required to bridge any gaps.

My aim is that you leave that call feeling clear, understood, and in control. No pressure, no jargon, no obligation.

Outside of work I'm usually on the water sailing, on a golf course, coaching and playing volleyball, or spending time with family.
